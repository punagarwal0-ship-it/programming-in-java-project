# Project Statement

**Project Title:** Inventory Management System  
**Student Name:** Puneet Agarwal  
**Registration No.:** 25BAI11166

## Problem Statement
Manual inventory tracking can cause incorrect stock counts, missed low-stock items, and difficulty maintaining product and supplier information. This project provides a simple console-based solution.

## Scope
The system manages products, stock quantities, prices, suppliers, low-stock alerts, reports, transaction history, and local persistent data.

## Target Users
- Small shop owners
- Store assistants
- Inventory managers
- Students learning Java

## High-Level Features
1. Product management
2. Stock management
3. Supplier management
4. Price management
5. Low-stock monitoring
6. Inventory reporting
7. Stock history
8. Local data persistence

## Input and Output
**Input:** Menu choices, product details, supplier details, prices, and stock quantities entered through the terminal.

**Output:** Product lists, alerts, supplier information, reports, transaction history, and validation/error messages.
